Logpoint  ·  Cybersecurity  ·  Brand, UX, UI

One website for engineers and buyers

Logpoint is a Nordic cybersecurity and SIEM provider. Its website spoke either to customers or to engineers, never both, and said little about its Nordic character. I redesigned its digital platform under one clear brand.

Role

Product Designer, Shoreditch Design Studio

Scope

Workshops, personas, flows, brand expression, UI, mini design system

Industry

Cybersecurity and IT analytics

Audience

Security leaders, technical experts, customers

The problem

The site leaned either customer facing or deeply technical, said little about Logpoint’s Nordic character, and did not show why its technology matters for security.

What I did

Aligned the team in workshops, built personas and flows, created minimal illustration and a mini design system, and tested with the CTO, Sales Director, Customer Success and customers.

The outcome

One clear brand and a site that works for customers and technical experts alike, with the Nordic character built into how it looks and feels.

01 The problem

A site that picked a side

The site leaned either toward customer content or toward technical detail, so the experience felt inconsistent. Logpoint wanted its Nordic culture, a closeness to nature and an inclusive spirit, to come through, but without losing the credibility a security buyer expects. The site also failed to show how advanced the technology is, and why it matters for cyber security, which called for evidence led pages and stronger imagery.

The brief was to align the brand, the culture and the business goals in a way that worked for every audience.

02 Align first

Workshops, personas and flows

I started with research into Nordic culture, Logpoint’s values and how it wanted nature to appear. Collaborative design thinking workshops with the Logpoint team created a shared vision and shared goals. I then built personas and user flows from the research, so the team could see the site through the eyes of a CISO, an analyst and a prospective customer.

03 Illustration

Friendly and secure at the same time

To serve a diverse audience while keeping the focus on the product, I chose minimal illustrations with no cultural emphasis. They bring warmth and a nature link, and they imply safety and trust, which is the main message. I sketched diverse characters to reinforce inclusivity and used the illustrations across the site for consistency.

The illustration system

Friendly, inclusive and unmistakably about security. Built as a set so any page can draw from it.

04 Mini design system

Consistency you can build on

A compact design system defined the colour palette, typography, iconography and UI components. It kept the redesign consistent and fast, and it carried the Nordic character into every part of the brand.

The design system

Type, colour and components, defined once and reused across every page.

05 Interaction

Motion with a serious tone

Interactive elements and subtle animation added engagement without undermining the credibility a security brand needs. Dynamic dark and light contrasts served the different needs of different audiences on the same site.

Testing with the people who sell and use it

Feedback came from the CTO, the Sales Director, Customer Success and customers. It changed the direction in clear ways.

What we heardDirection
Strengthen the connection with nature in the brandNature themes carried further through illustration and imagery.
Some rewording, or a mindset shiftContent reframed around customer outcomes, not product features.
More focus on industriesIndustry led content so visitors see their own world reflected.
Photos of people and illustrations for webinarsBoth used, so events feel human as well as on brand.
Sharper, stronger, more impactful illustrationsA refined illustration style with more confidence.
06 Delivery

What shipped

A redesigned website with a user friendly interface, a cohesive brand, engaging illustration and UI examples showing how the new identity applies to real pages. One clear brand now speaks to customers and technical experts alike, and the Nordic character is part of how the product feels.
