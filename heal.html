Heal  ·  Health  ·  Founder project

A way for people the system could not diagnose to explore alternative treatment

Heal is a digital platform for people frustrated by traditional medicine. It compares treatments on cost and efficacy and includes a booking system. I founded and designed it after my own experience of unsatisfactory support for an unknown illness.

Audience

The problem

People whose doctors cannot explain their symptoms want other options, but are overwhelmed and worried about cost, trust and whether anything will work.

What I did

Surveyed 88 people, interviewed people with chronic illness, built an MVP with a chatbot, medical history and doctor assignment, then tested and iterated.

The outcome

In testing, 31% valued the simpler healing process, 16% the clinic search and 13% the reminders. It has not launched yet and is still being refined.

01 The problem

Overwhelmed, and worried about money

Someone whose doctors cannot explain their symptoms wants to explore other options. They are overwhelmed by choices, willing to try several approaches, and anxious about spending money on the wrong one. They need help finding the treatment that fits their case, at a price they can justify.

02 Research

Listening before building

I surveyed 88 people and interviewed people living with chronic or unexplained illness. Concerns clustered around effectiveness (43%), cost (29%) and trust (11%), and 51% reported an illness their doctors had not identified. The message was consistent: people need accessible, trustworthy options.

I put every answer on a board and grouped it by theme: work and lifestyle, experience and opinions, possible problems, relationship with technology, and the booking process people already know.

05 Ideation

Two exercises, many voices

I ran brainwriting and Crazy 8’s with three participants, so the ideas did not all come from me. The strongest themes were a doctor matched to each patient, one medical history that travels with them, and honest reviews from people who had been through it.

09 The MVP

Heal, built around those concerns

  • A user friendly website and a chatbot that guides people through their options.
  • Medical history tracking and a doctor assignment system.
  • Progress tracking and a peer review system, so people see what worked for others like them.
