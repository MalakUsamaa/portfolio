A little biography

From buildings to screens

I started in design and architecture at the University of Melbourne and graduated in graphic design, but the way architects think about structure, space and flow never left. Seven years on, I've designed across fintech, healthtech and edutech, from lending and mortgage journeys to brand identities and design systems, working across Europe, the Middle East and Australia. I now run Mpavida.

I'm naturally curious, a little restless, and always looking for something that makes me see things differently. I feel things deeply, think about things endlessly, and rarely take the ordinary route. I love beautiful details, meaningful connections, new experiences, and the feeling of being completely absorbed in something. Above all, I'm someone who never wants to stop discovering who I can become.
