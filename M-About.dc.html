About me

Clear and honest, calm when it matters

I am a product designer with seven years in lending and regulated fintech. I design the moments where people hand over sensitive information, make a big financial decision or wait for an answer, and I make sure those moments are clear, honest and calm.

My work sits where design, regulation and business meet. I read the rules behind a requirement, ask what problem a brief is really solving and design the journey from that, then test it with the people who will use it.

I have worked on mortgages, credit and lending products across Europe, the Middle East and Australia, led a design team, built an enterprise design system and now run Mpavida, a product design consultancy for fintech, hospitality and SaaS startups.
